# budget-web-app

Expense Tracker

A modern, secure, and user-friendly web application for tracking your daily expenses. Designed to feel just like a native mobile app, it's always available when you need it, even offline.

Features

    Secure User Accounts: Keep your financial data private with secure user registration and login.

    Full Expense Management: Easily create, read, edit, and delete your expense records.

    Detailed Records: Store an amount, category, date, and an optional comment for each expense.

    Data Visualization: See where your money is going with visual graphs and charts.

    Powerful Filtering: View statistics for specific periods (day, week, month, or year) and filter your expenses by category.

    Installable App (PWA): Install the app directly on your mobile device or desktop for a seamless, native-like experience.

    Offline Functionality: Record new expenses even when you don't have an internet connection.

How to Use

    Create an account or log in with your credentials.

    Start adding your expenses on the main dashboard.

    Visit the "Stats" page to see a breakdown of your spending habits.

    Follow your browser's prompt to "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen" for easy access.
